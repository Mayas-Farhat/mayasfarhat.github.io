## 🔬 Featured Projects

### Biophysics
* **Biophysics mTOR:** Research presentation on protein Binder design using the Bindcraft pipeline (AI using AlphaFold2).
    * [View Presentation](./Biophysics_mTOR_PRESENTATION.pdf)
* **Genome Statistics & Markov Models of Evolution:** ORF-length distributions in the *E. coli* genome against a random-sequence null; a four-state Markov model of nucleotide substitution solved by symmetry (steady state and relaxation time); base-pair correlation decay; and a master equation for a two-state reaction.
    * [View Solutions](./biophys_HMW_1_SOLUTIONS-Mayas_Farhat.pdf)
* **Population Dynamics & the Wright–Fisher Model:** A bacterial growth-and-dilution experiment mapped onto the Wright–Fisher process; the forward Kolmogorov (Fokker–Planck) equation solved for the allele-frequency distribution; steady states, absorbing fixation/loss, selection versus genetic drift, and fixation probabilities.
    * [View Solutions](./biophys_HMW_2-Mayas_Farhat.pdf)
* **Fixation Dynamics & Sequence Alignment:** The multi-allele Wright–Fisher model with forward/backward Kolmogorov equations and mean-fixation-time (first-passage) analysis; splitting probabilities and mean exit times for diffusion with absorbing boundaries in 1–3D; and the combinatorics and dynamic-programming optimal alignment of gapped sequences.
    * [View Solutions](./Biophys%20Hmw%203.pdf)
